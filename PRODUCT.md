# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is English-speaking users in overseas markets who know AI may help with a task but need plain-language guidance to choose a suitable product. A secondary audience is experienced AI users comparing pricing, capabilities, platforms, and alternatives.

## Product Purpose

Help people discover, evaluate, and confidently choose AI tools, then continue to the official product website. Search-focused guides, comparisons, pricing pages, and tutorials acquire users and establish trust; tool detail pages convert that intent into qualified outbound visits.

## Positioning

A decision platform rather than a link directory: beginner-friendly task discovery backed by structured, recently verified product data, transparent ranking criteria, and practical comparisons.

## Operating Context

Users arrive from English-language search results, browse by task or search directly, compare a small set of tools, inspect pricing and limitations, and follow a clearly disclosed link to the official website.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing Next.js site with a large tool dataset, categories, scenarios, rankings, tutorials, news, guides, glossary, and English/Simplified Chinese/Traditional Chinese localization.
- English is the primary market and should become the default language; Chinese remains available as a secondary language.
- Prices should prioritize USD and decision-relevant facts for overseas users.
- Existing factual content and tool data must be preserved unless corrected with evidence.
- Mobile responsiveness, accessibility, performance, and transparent affiliate disclosure are required.
- Affiliate links and advertising must never silently influence editorial rankings or ratings.

## Brand Commitments

The current working name is AINav / AI Navigation. A distinct international brand name and production domain remain open decisions. The new interface should feel like a credible technology intelligence product, not a generic purple-gradient AI template.

## Evidence on Hand

- Structured records for hundreds of AI tools in `data/tools.json` and supporting application code.
- Tool logos in `public/logos/`.
- Tutorials, glossary entries, news sources, scenarios, pricing fields, traffic fields, and comparison-oriented content in the project.
- No testimonials, verified customer claims, or finalized affiliate partnerships are currently established and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Beginner-friendly entry, expert-grade evidence.
- Every page should shorten the path from a real task to a confident tool choice.
- Trust is earned through sources, methodology, timestamps, and disclosures.
- Commercial content serves user fit first; affiliate revenue is secondary to editorial integrity.
- English SEO and high-intent decision content take priority over broad, short-lived news coverage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The website must support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, semantic structure, reduced motion preferences, and responsive layouts without horizontal overflow.
