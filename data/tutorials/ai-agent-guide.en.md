AI agents take you from "chatting" with AI to "getting work done" by AI. This guide explains what agents are and how to build your own.

## What is an agent?

A regular chatbot answers one question at a time, like a consultant. An agent actually gets work done, like an employee: you give it a goal, and it breaks it into steps, calls tools, and finishes the job.

Think of it this way: a chatbot tells you how to cook a dish; an agent cooks the whole meal.

## Three things that make agents work

- **Planning**: breaking a big goal into small steps
- **Tool calling**: looking things up or taking action when needed
- **Memory**: remembering context and your preferences

## Build your own (no code needed)

Use Coze (coze.cn, ByteDance's free platform):

1. Create a bot
2. Describe its role and task
3. Add plugins (tools) like web search or image generation
4. Test and publish it

## What agents are good at (and not)

Good for fixed, repetitive tasks and content generation. Not yet reliable for money-related or high-stakes decisions. Treat an agent as a helpful assistant, not a fully autonomous employee.
