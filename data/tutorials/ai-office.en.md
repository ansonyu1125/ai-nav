Documents, PPTs, and meeting minutes are the most time-consuming repetitive tasks. This tutorial teaches you how to use AI tools to delegate these tasks to AI, saving time for more important things.

## 1. Writing Documents with AI

- **Notion AI**: Press space in a note to activate, can summarize, continue writing, translate, and polish.
- **WPS AI**: One-click generation of document outlines, paragraph continuation, and full-text translation in WPS.
- Usage: Provide a title and key points, let it generate a draft, then manually refine details.

## 2. Creating PPTs with AI

**Gamma** is the most efficient choice, visit [gamma.app](https://gamma.app):

1. Enter a topic, such as "2026 Annual Product Planning Report".
2. It will automatically generate complete slides with charts.
3. Then adjust text, colors, and layout as needed.

WPS AI can also generate a PPT outline based on document content with one click.

## 3. Organizing Meeting Minutes with AI

- **Feishu Intelligent Partner**: Automatically generates minutes, to-dos, and action items after meetings.
- **DingTalk AI**: Supports group chat summaries and document Q&A.
- After transcribing audio to text, let AI extract "conclusions + to-dos + responsible persons".

## 4. Efficient Workflow

1. Let AI produce a **draft** first, rather than starting from scratch.
2. Use AI for **review**: check typos, optimize expression, and unify tone.
3. For repetitive templates (weekly reports, notices, emails), directly solidify them into prompt templates.

> Reminder: When AI-generated content involves sensitive company information, pay attention to data security and permission boundaries.
