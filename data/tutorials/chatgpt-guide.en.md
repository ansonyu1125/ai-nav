ChatGPT is a conversational AI assistant developed by OpenAI and is the AI product with the largest user base globally. This tutorial helps you get started from registration and quickly master its core usage.

## 1. Registration and Access

Visit the official website [chatgpt.com](https://chatgpt.com) and register with your email or Google account. The free version provides basic conversation features; upgrading to Plus gives you access to more powerful models and image generation capabilities.

> Tip: It is recommended to bind a frequently used email during registration for easier account recovery. Users in China may need to pay attention to the network environment when accessing.

## 2. Basic Conversations

Type your question directly in the input box and press Enter to send. You can:

- Ask knowledge questions, such as "What is quantum entanglement? Explain it in simple terms."
- Ask it to write, such as "Help me write a leave request email."
- Ask it to translate, summarize, or rewrite text.

Conversations support multiple turns, and ChatGPT remembers the context. To start a new topic, click "New Chat."

## 3. Advanced Features

1. **Web Search**: When enabled, ChatGPT can search for real-time information and cite sources.
2. **File Upload**: Upload images, PDFs, or documents for analysis or summarization.
3. **Image Generation**: Plus users can generate images directly or edit uploaded images.
4. **Voice Conversations**: The app supports voice input and real-time voice interaction.

## 4. Practical Tips

- The more specific your request, the more accurate the response. Replace "Write a plan" with "Write a 7-day AI learning plan for college students, 30 minutes per day."
- If you are not satisfied, directly ask it to "make it shorter" or "use a more formal tone."
- For important information, ask it to "provide sources" and verify key facts, as AI can sometimes hallucinate.
