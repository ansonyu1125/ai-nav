Can AI make you money? Yes — but not by "lying flat." AI is an amplifier: it makes work you already know how to do faster, so you can take on more. Here are five realistic directions.

## 1. AI writing / content

Use AI to produce articles, social posts, and video scripts faster. You pick the topic and tone; AI drafts.

## 2. AI design / image editing

Use tools like Midjourney or Jimeng to make avatars, posters, and product images for clients.

## 3. AI video / short-form video

Use AI for scripts, visuals, and voiceover to produce short videos at scale.

## 4. AI coding / freelance

Use Cursor to build small sites, apps, and tools much faster. Highest ceiling, but requires coding skill.

## 5. AI teaching / services

If you use AI well, teach others: make tutorials, run training, or offer "done-for-you" AI services.

## Bottom line

Pick one direction and go deep. AI amplifies skills you already have — it can't create skills out of nothing.
