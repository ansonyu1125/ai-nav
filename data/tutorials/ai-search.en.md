AI search has transformed "keyword retrieval" into "direct answers." This tutorial teaches you how to use Perplexity and Metaso AI search to efficiently obtain reliable information with sources.

## 1. AI Search vs Traditional Search

Traditional search returns a bunch of links for you to filter through, while AI search directly summarizes the answer and cites the sources:

- **Direct answers**: Get the conclusion in one sentence.
- **Verifiable sources**: Every piece of information can be clicked back to the original text for verification.
- **Multi-turn follow-ups**: You can continue to dig deeper based on the results.

## 2. Using Perplexity

Visit [perplexity.ai](https://www.perplexity.ai), register, and you're ready to use it:

1. Ask directly, e.g., "What are the most noteworthy AI trends in 2026?"
2. Use `Focus` to switch the search scope (web, academic, video, etc.)
3. Click "Sources" to view the sources and verify key conclusions.
4. Follow up with "Tell me more about the first point" to get deeper answers.

## 3. Metaso AI Search

Visit [metaso.cn](https://metaso.cn) - great Chinese experience, no ads:

- **Concise / In-depth / Research** three modes; the research mode generates a structured in-depth report.
- **Academic search**: Ideal for finding papers and literature.
- **Source tracing**: Every citation can be clicked to view the original text for comparison.

## 4. Practical Tips

- For comparisons, ask "What's the difference between A and B?" and it will list them in a table.
- When looking up data, add "Give specific data and sources" to avoid vague statements.
- Always click into the original source to verify important information, as AI summaries may have inaccuracies.

> Tip: AI search is great for "finding answers" and "doing research," but for critical decisions, cross-verify with multiple sources.
