DeepSeek is a free, powerful AI assistant from the Chinese company DeepSeek (深度求索). This guide explains what it is and how to use it.

## What is DeepSeek?

A free chatbot you can use like ChatGPT — ask questions, write, translate, summarize, code, and reason. Key traits:

- Free with no subscription
- Strong Chinese and reasoning ability
- Open-source model

## Getting started

Open chat.deepseek.com or the mobile app, sign up, and start chatting.

## Two modes

- **Deep Think (R1)**: reasons first, great for math, logic, and coding
- **Normal chat**: fast, good for everyday use

Switch to Deep Think for hard problems, and normal mode for quick questions.

## Practical prompts

- **Writing**: describe what you need, including audience, length, and tone
- **Coding**: paste the error and ask it to explain and fix it
- **Summarizing**: paste a long article and ask for five bullet points

## Watch out for

It can still hallucinate, has a knowledge cutoff (turn on web search for recent info), and you should avoid pasting sensitive information.
