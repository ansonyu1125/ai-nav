Text-to-video is one of the most popular AI capabilities in the past two years. This tutorial introduces mainstream tools such as Sora and Kling, and teaches you to generate your first AI video.

## 1. Comparison of Mainstream Tools

- **Sora** (OpenAI): The benchmark for quality, capable of generating high-quality videos, requires a ChatGPT subscription.
- **Kling AI** (Kuaishou): Performs well in Chinese contexts, with smooth motion and friendly free quota.
- **Runway**: Geared towards professional creation and video editing, with comprehensive features.
- **Hailuo AI** (MiniMax): Fast generation speed, supports voice.

## 2. Generate Your First Video

Take Kling as an example. Visit [klingai.com](https://klingai.com) to register, then enter a description:

> A golden retriever running in an autumn park, leaves swirling in the wind, sunlight filtering through the treetops, cinematic shot

Choose the duration and aspect ratio, click generate, and wait from tens of seconds to a few minutes.

## 3. Prompt Writing Tips

- **Subject + Action**: Clearly state "who is doing what".
- **Environment and Atmosphere**: Time, place, lighting.
- **Camera Language**: Camera movement (push, pull, pan, tilt), shot size, style.
- **Duration and Aspect Ratio**: Vertical for short videos, horizontal for films.

### Example

> Slow push-in, an astronaut walking on the lunar surface, Earth rising in the distance, photorealistic style, cinematic quality

## 4. Advanced Techniques

1. **Image-to-Video**: First create a keyframe image, then let the AI animate it for better control.
2. **Segment Generation**: Break long videos into segments, generate them separately, then edit and stitch together.
3. **Try Multiple Times**: Video generation has randomness; generate several versions of the same prompt and pick the best.

> Tip: Pay attention to copyright and content compliance; do not generate real people's likenesses or infringing material.
