You don't need to know music theory or play an instrument to create a complete song with AI. This tutorial will guide you through generating music from scratch using Suno.

## 1. Getting to Know Suno

Suno is currently the most popular AI music generation tool. Enter a description or lyrics, and it will generate a complete song with vocals and accompaniment. Visit [suno.com](https://suno.com) to sign up; the free tier is enough to try it out.

## 2. Two Creation Methods

### 1. Description Generation

Directly describe the music you want:

> A light folk song about summer and the seaside, with acoustic guitar, female vocals, healing style

### 2. Lyrics Generation

In "Custom" mode, paste or write lyrics, specify the style, and the AI will compose music for your lyrics.

## 3. Tips for Writing Lyrics

- Clear structure: Mark verses, choruses, and bridges with `[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`
- Repetitive chorus: The chorus is the hook; make the lyrics catchy
- Clear style: Specify genre, mood, instruments, and language in the prompt

### Example Lyrics

> [Verse] Evening breeze blows through old streets / Streetlights stretch the shadows long
> [Chorus] That summer we promised / To go to distant places together

## 4. Generation and Iteration

1. Click generate, and Suno will return two versions for you to listen to.
2. Choose a satisfactory version and click "Continue" to extend the song's length.
3. If you're not satisfied with the style, modify the style description in the prompt and regenerate.
