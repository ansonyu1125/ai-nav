Midjourney is one of the most popular text-to-image tools, known for its artistic quality. This tutorial takes you from zero to generating your first satisfying image.

## 1. Registration and Usage

Visit [midjourney.com](https://www.midjourney.com) to register an account. Midjourney is subscription-based, and you can also use it via Discord commands. Beginners are advised to try the web version first, as it is more intuitive.

## 2. Generate Your First Image

Enter a prompt in the input box, for example:

> An orange cat sitting by the window, afternoon sunlight, cozy interior, photographic style, shallow depth of field

After pressing Enter, wait a moment and you'll get four candidate images. Click on the one you like to enlarge it or generate variations.

## 3. Prompt Patterns

A complete Midjourney prompt usually includes:

- **Subject**: What to draw (person, animal, scene)
- **Style**: Photography, illustration, oil painting, cyberpunk, Studio Ghibli style, etc.
- **Lighting and Atmosphere**: Backlight, neon, morning mist, warmth
- **Parameters**: Such as `--ar 16:9` to control aspect ratio, `--v 6` to specify version

### Example

> A futuristic city floating in the clouds, cyberpunk style, neon lights, dusk, detailed, --ar 16:9

## 4. Advanced Tips

1. Use "Image-to-Image": Upload a reference image and let AI generate based on its style.
2. Learn "Weights": `flower::2 mountain::1` to emphasize the flower in the image.
3. Iterate repeatedly: Click "Variation" on a satisfactory image to gradually approach the ideal result.

> Tip: Before generating an image, think about its purpose (avatar, poster, wallpaper). Aspect ratio and style directly affect the result.
